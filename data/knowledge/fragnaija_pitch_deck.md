## What is Frag Naija?
FragNaija is Nigeria’s dedicated esports and competitive gaming platform combining organized tournaments, live prediction systems, community rankings, and a mobile-first experience built for the Nigerian gaming ecosystem. Built with Next.js 14, Supabase, and Paystack, and deployed as a scalable Progressive Web App (PWA) designed to support the next generation of African gamers from day one.

Core Pillars:
- Tournaments: Structured competitive brackets
- Wager Markets: House-favored prediction & outcome wagering
- Leaderboards: Real-time player & clan rankings nationwide
- Community: Nigeria’s largest esports hub combining organized tournaments, live prediction systems, community rankings, and a mobile-first experience tailored for the Nigerian gaming ecosystem.

## Platform Features
- Tournament Engine: Structured Tournament Brackets, Admin-managed match creation & scoring, Tiered entry and prize distribution.
- Wager & Predictions: House-favored outcome markets, Paystack deposit & withdrawal rails, Real-time odds and market resolution.
- Rankings & Stats: Nation-wide Power Rankings, Player stat tracking per tournament, Clan and squad performance boards.
- Mobile-First PWA: Installable Progressive Web App, Offline-ready core features, Optimised for low-bandwidth Nigerian networks.

## Business Model (How We Make Money)
- Wager House Edge: Every prediction market carries a built-in house margin. As volume scales, this becomes the primary revenue engine.
- Tournament Entry Fees: Tiered entry fees per tournament bracket. FragNaija takes a platform cut from every competition entry.
- Brand Sponsorships: Esports brands and consumer companies pay for tournament naming rights, in-app placements, and community access.
- Premium Features: Priority matchmaking, advanced stats dashboards, and clan management tools as freemium upsells.

## Payment Infrastructure
- Paystack Deposits: Instant card & bank transfers into wallet.
- Paystack Withdrawals: Verified payout rails to user bank accounts.
- Wallet System: In-app balance for seamless wagering UX.
- Transaction Cuts: Platform fee on every in-app transaction.

## Technology Stack
- Frontend: Next.js 14 (App Router), React Admin Panel, Tailwind CSS, PWA (installable on mobile).
- Backend & Database: Supabase (PostgreSQL), Row-level security policies, Real-time subscriptions, Edge functions for logic.
- Payments: Paystack integration, Deposit & withdrawal flows, Webhook event handling, Wallet balance management.
- Infrastructure: Vercel deployment, CI/CD pipeline, Global CDN edge, Scalable from launch day.
